Related Party Radar

Ask about any large US public company. A team of research agents reads its latest proxy statement and annual report for deals with its own insiders, and works out which of those deals cross a line.

What it explores

Related-party transactions: business between a company and its directors, executives, 5% holders or their families. Think a director's other company selling to this one, or an executive's son on the payroll.

Who it's for

Shareholders deciding how to vote on directors, governance and stewardship analysts, and reporters who want the deals and the arithmetic, with every fact linked to the sentence in the filing it came from.

How to use it

  1. Name a company (Russell 1000) and what you want to know.
  2. Watch the researchers work in parallel (about 2 minutes).
  3. Read the verdict. Click any source tag to jump to that passage in the filing on sec.gov.
